Posted by Sharbani Roy – Senior Director, Product Management, Google We're back with the third annual Women in Machine Learning Symposium on December 7, 2023 ! Join us virtually from 9:30 am to 1:00 pm PT for an immersive and insightful set of deep dives for every level of Machine Learning experience. The Women in ML Symposium is an inclusive event for anyone passionate about the transformative fields of Machine Learning (ML) and Artificial Intelligence (AI).
