Posted by Paul Ruiz , Developer Relations Engineer We're excited to announce that the TensorFlow Lite plugin for Flutter has been officially migrated to the TensorFlow GitHub account and released! Three years ago, Amish Garg , one of our talented Google Summer of Code contributors, wrote a widely used TensorFlow Lite plugin for Flutter. The plugin was so popular that we decided to migrate it to our official repo, making it easier to maintain directly by the Google team.
