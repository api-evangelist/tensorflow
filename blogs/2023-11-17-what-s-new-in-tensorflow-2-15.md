Posted by the TensorFlow team TensorFlow 2.15 has been released! Highlights of this release (and 2.14) include a much simpler installation method for NVIDIA CUDA libraries for Linux, oneDNN CPU performance optimizations for Windows x64 and x86, full availability of tf.function types, an upgrade to Clang 17.0.1, and much more!
